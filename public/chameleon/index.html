    How to play

    Become the room.

    Hide mode

    • Move: WASD, jump Space, drag to look, scroll to zoom.
    • Paint: press Tab. Click scenery to sample it, then drag across your body. Drag empty space or right-drag to orbit.
    • Pattern: sample bricks, planks or wallpaper and switch the source to Pattern. It paints in line with the real surface, so press up against it.
    • Pose: 1–7, turn with Q E. Lie flat to crawl under beds.
    • Check: C shows how you look from the angle where you stand out most. Enter starts the hunt.
    • Seekers notice colour mismatch, your shadow, and any movement.

    Seek mode

    • Click the view to lock the mouse, then look around. WASD to move, Shift to run.
    • Fire paint: left click. Misses cost 3 seconds.
    • Zoom: hold right mouse or Z.
    • Sonar: Q points toward the nearest hider. It recharges after 25 seconds.
    • Hiders painted themselves from one viewpoint. Walk around: from other angles the seams show.
